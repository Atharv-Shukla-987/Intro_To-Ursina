It is action shooter game , something like real life where u can not win and will loose eventually or just leave because u are bored as hell

## Controls

Movement -------- 
Forward - press W
Backward - press S
Right - press D
Left - press A

crouch to heal yourself - press Shift

fire bullets - Click Mouse left

To Change Weapon - Mouse Scroll


## How to play

You just need to download the main.zip from here and after exracting it open , the main executable file and after it you are ready to play !!!
